Why ABN Group

We are not optimizing
the old marketing model.
We are built on a new one.

The marketing industry is going through the deepest shift in its history. Data, omnichannel and artificial intelligence are redefining how brands grow, acquire users and build value.

ABN Group was born inside this new paradigm. It is not a company adapting to the transformation. It was designed from day one to operate in this context, with data, technology and performance as its structural foundation.

ABN Group is not an agency. It is an integrated growth system that brings media, data, technology and creativity together under a single operating logic. Through its units — ABN Digital, Hike, Detrics and ABN Studio — the group covers the full cycle end to end: acquisition, measurement, optimization and scale.

That integration unlocks something few companies achieve: executing with speed, learning with depth and scaling with consistency.

01

A system of execution and continuous improvement

Every process, decision and result is documented and structured. This builds a compounding asset of knowledge that sharpens execution over time, reduces errors and lets the group scale while keeping standards high. It is not just experience. It is a system that learns.

02

A high-standards culture with real autonomy

Clear, measurable standards of excellence are shared across every team, combined with a culture of autonomy, accountability and transparency that sustains speed without losing control. Talent density and execution capacity are a defining edge.

03

Structural adoption of technology and AI

Artificial intelligence is not an add-on. It is part of how the business runs. The group develops internal capabilities continuously — AI School, Tech Talks, training systems — while building proprietary technology it can use and commercialize, creating operating leverage.

Where many marketing companies face structural limits — people-intensive models, low standardization, difficulty incorporating technology — ABN runs on a different architecture: lighter, more integrated and built to scale.

It combines the best of two worlds: the execution capacity of a services company with the asset-building logic of a technology company.

ABN is not optimizing the traditional marketing model. It is built on a new one.
